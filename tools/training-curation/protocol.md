## Representation resolution

`plan.json` binds this protocol and the executable sources before selection IDs are produced. Input-only normalization uses all 906 source records, including the quarantined case's undisputed authored input. Each column has unit weight within each feature family when computing percentiles. Effective scale is the larger of the p05–p95 span and 10% of the full range. A numerically constant family requires equality within 1e-12 times the magnitude or one. Scale constants and transformations are not learned from a model.

Keep absolute feed scale and component fractions, temperatures, pressures, reflux, specific duties, individual device placement/rates and native-grid source/cumulative arrays. Pumparounds contribute authored stage heat only. The complete physical profiles use native nodes, not interpolated stage counts. Each pair must have matching property/component/formulation contract, exact stage count, branch, active-feed set, equipment counts, ordered heat placement rules and relevant zero/sign categories.

Every continuous input difference must be at most 5% of its effective scale. Maximum native temperature difference is 2 K. Phase traffic/feed difference must be at most 0.02 + 0.05 times the larger value at every node. Phase composition total variation is at most 0.02 wherever both phase totals are positive. Above-floor component flows differ by at most 0.25 decades after dividing by each case's floor. Floors are `1e-10 * max(component feed, 1e-12 * total feed)`. Exact zero, positive below floor, one-to-ten floors and at-least-ten floors are distinct bands; structural absences and water/wet behavior must agree. Stored labels are never thresholded or rewritten. Sensitivity multiplies continuous tolerances by 0.5 and 2; categorical, structural and trace protections stay fixed.
