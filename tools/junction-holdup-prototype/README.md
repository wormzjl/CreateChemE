## Purpose

Tests two things. E1: a Jacobian check of the zero-holdup junction mixing rows. E2: whether a regularised junction mixing closure can replace the cold-start pressure seed and carry finite-tank runs through rest. Every prototype switch is a system property; each one's default reproduces unchanged behaviour.

- `holdup-prototype-stageform.patch` (2026-09-25, runs 54-64; eight tracked files: the seven above plus PipeTransfer): the mint patch plus the stage-one switch of review 7.9. Apply it to a clean 9674bf1 **instead of** the other patches (it contains them; `git apply --check` on the restored base and `--check --reverse` on the patched tree verified). On top of mint it adds:
  - `junction.stageForm` (Gradle `-PstageForm=`, default `explicit` = unchanged; `implicit` needs `-PrateForm=frozen` and a holdup). `implicit`: TR-BDF2 re-books the endpoint rate's cached flows for every step with `ConservativeTransport.reconstruct(portGraph, rate states, flows, heads, 1, ..., frozen, relax = alpha*dt)`. Each owned junction (no scheduled transfer, not on a junction cycle in flow direction) books its outflows as its share |q_e|/Q_out of dt Q_out S1/m, where S1 = (S0 + relax In)/(1 + relax Q_out/m) is its stock advanced by backward Euler over alpha*dt with everything its inflows delivered (species, solid populations, energy field, pump work); edges are visited junction-upstream first. So the junction's stage-one base S0 + alpha dt (In - Out) is exactly S1 >= 0, relax -> 0 is the frozen rate (consistent: the rate at the stock's own value) and relax -> infinity a pass-through. Ports and boundaries book the same amounts; the pipe history of the explicit term is re-weighted the same way (`PipeTransfer.relaxed`: a*frozen sample + b*share*inflow streams). The endpoint-rate cache now also keeps the device heads (pump work of the re-booking). The rate solve, its flows, states and cache keys are unchanged. Under `implicit` every frozen booking (also the rate solve's own, relax = 0) forms the junction pseudo-boundary from the booked tallies **with solids** (populations gained: direction -1; lost: a second transfer, direction +1), which fixes the "Solid population balance failed" of the filter-to-void gates (review 7.8 defect 2). The stage-two base is unchanged (`n0 + A (n1 - n0)`, A = 1.2071, not convex).
  - `junction.stageFormTrace` (Gradle `-PstageFormTrace=true`, prints only): TR-BDF2 records each step's junction mass change split into the endpoint-rate term A(base1 - n0), stage one A(n1 - base1) and stage two n2 - base2; `PassiveIntervalSolver.junctionBudget` sums the accepted steps; the transient probe appends ` budget=[rate, stage1, stage2]` (kg since the case began) to its trajectory lines.
