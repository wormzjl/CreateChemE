# F1: pumped fills held for minutes or forever - root cause, fix, hold policy, pump proposal

Date: 2026-09-24. Batch `2026-09-23-fluid-followups`, package F1 (F2 placement cost and F3 checkpoint storage are other agents'). Branch `claude/fluid-followups` in worktree `agent-ae139e4fc1b184b36`, over `main` @ `23beadd` (0.3.0). Not merged, not pushed. Every number below is in `f1-tables.md`; raw material in `f1-logs/` (traces, gate logs, paced reports, in-game runs), the probes and the rig copy in `probes/` and `f1-rig/`.

**Windows: in-game 60 s warm-up after placement + 60 s measured, soaks 60 s + 300 s; paced 60 s + 60 s.**

## 0. Summary

* **Root cause, three sentences.** Water pumped into a chain of dry nitrogen tanks evaporates into the first tank, cools it about 17 K to its wet-bulb temperature within milliseconds, and turns every connection downstream around; the Newton step linearizes a vessel's transported amounts on the donor of the current flow sign, so it predicted that the second tank loses the first tank's steam, which it holds only as a trace, and the nonnegativity step limiter then cut the whole island's step to that trace's size (2e-10, 2e-12, ...) until the line search stalled - and the velocity clamp, whose donor switched at zero flow, put a second jump into the same reversals. A held attempt left its retained solver warm-started from wherever the attempt had stopped inside the slice, so every retry of the same committed state could fail identically forever, and the hold policy retried it every cadence at the full wall budget, halving the slice only for wall cuts, from 100 ticks down, 2 s of thrown-away work per level. Gas transfers between closed tanks are different: the suction tank cools adiabatically to the fluid model's 273.16 K floor after 21.9 s, and no state of the model lies beyond it.
* **Fixed (numerics, in the solver):** a trace below a millionth of its difference floor no longer bounds the step length and is projected instead (`ee050d4`); a plain passive connection's velocity cap is read off its driving pressure (`55ba5a0`). **Fixed (policy, in the coordinator):** a fresh solver after every hold, every hold halves the slice, one-tick holds back off to at most 64 cadences, a refused soft-budget fallback is retried as a full solve (`5b3d903`); an island a topology change created starts on a one-tick slice (`7f933ff`).
* **In game (dedicated server):** `fill100` 12.2 to 0.35 cores at 60+60 s, 0 held warnings, every line FULL by online tick 800; the 300 s soak 6.87 to 0.32 cores, **100 of 100** lines filled to the pump's shutoff and certified STEADY (before 67, 33 held for good). Six-tank fills, fill variants and vented chains (probes 1, 2, 4) run with no hold at the end of the window (2.0 / 7.6 / 2.3 to 0.58 / 0.89 / 0.24 cores). Gas transfers (probe 3) still stop at the model's floor; their retries back off, so 24 held transfers cost 0.1 to 0.2 cores after the first minute.
* **Pump model:** not involved in the fills (the pump stays at its target through every failure and closes cleanly at its shutoff). It is involved in the gas transfers. Section 5 proposes three simplifications; the recommended one changes what the "Max pressure rise" field means, so it is a proposal for the owner and was not implemented.
* **Gates:** science 161/0, runtime 202/0, network 30/9, 19/14, 37/3, regression exactly zero (no re-capture), GameTests 28 plus 1 new (29), P12/P31 byte-identical.

## 2. Root cause with evidence (T2)

A temporary trace sink (per attempt: time, step, outcome; per active-set pass: modes, closures, phases, start flows; per Newton iteration: norm, worst row, step length, backtracks, the column that limited the step) was added for the investigation only, saved as `f1-logs/wip-with-trace-instrumentation.patch`, and removed before any commit. Traces are in `f1-logs/trace/`; `profile.js`, `failures.js` and `limits.js` classify them.

### 2.1 The physics of the start: evaporative cooling and reversal

At t = 7.7 ms of the three-tank fill the pump has delivered 0.077 kg of water; the first tank holds 6.9e-5 m3 of it, is at 281.3 K and 96,826 Pa, and the connections from the second to the first tank and from the third to the second run backwards at the velocity limit, -0.22 kg/s. That is the model's equilibrium physics and it is right: the first splash of water into 1 m3 of dry nitrogen evaporates until the gas is saturated, and its latent heat takes the tank to the adiabatic saturation (wet-bulb) temperature of dry gas at 298 K, about 283 K; the gas contracts and the downstream tanks push nitrogen back. As more water arrives the tank warms again, is compressed, and wet gas moves downstream into tanks that cool in turn; each tank's first water produces the same event, and every inter-tank connection reverses one or more times in the first two seconds.

### 2.2 Mechanism A (numerics): a steam trace pins the step length

`PassiveStepSolver.Equations.maximumStep` bounds a step so no amount unknown goes negative (`alpha <= .99*x/-d`). A vessel's transported amounts are linearized with the donor of the flow sign at the current iterate, so a direction that reverses a connection predicts that the far vessel loses the near vessel's composition. The second tank holds steam only as a trace: the `1e-12` entry trace `initialPhaseSeeds` plants for every reachable component, or 1e-14 to 1e-32 that an earlier reversal carried there. Measured at the six-tank chain's first stall: column 10 (the second tank's steam, x = 1e-12) with direction -4.43e-3 set the step length to 2.23e-10, then 2.23e-12, 2.23e-14, 2.23e-16 in successive iterations, and the line search reported a stall at 1.023 with every other unknown frozen. Over the three-tank chain's first interval 73 of 76 Newton failures were this limiter on the second or third tank's steam; the six-tank chain's first interval 14 of 19; its deterministic in-game retry after one committed tick (`0.096...`) 20 of 20. The same defect family was recorded as latent in the solid-phase batch (`DEAD_HEADED_LINE.md`: "the step limiter's treatment of an unknown whose only root is zero"); the pumped fills are where it is live.

**Numerics, not physics:** the trace's own row is scaled by the node's whole amount (a gas node, or a species the node did not hold at the start of the step), so the trace moves no row the Newton converges on by more than 1e-10, and the step the limiter refused was otherwise a good Newton step.

### 2.3 Mechanism B (numerics): the velocity clamp's donor switched at zero flow

The throttled-inlet law replaces a connection's hydraulic row by `(+-limit - flow)/limit` when the driving pressure exceeds the pressure drop at the donor's velocity limit, and the donor was the live flow's. Where the driving pressure lies between the two ends' limit drops, the row jumps at exactly zero flow. Measured at t = 0.128 s of the three-tank fill, on the third-to-second-tank connection: driving -2,343 Pa, start flow +2.9e-188 (the previous point's), row -0.0234 on the forward side and 0.99998 an alpha of 1.2e-7 later; all 24 backtracks land on the plateau, `Newton line search stalled at residual 0.10479353009252752`. In game this is what held all 100 `fill100` lines right after their first committed tick (`0.10479996312241754`, 100 times in one period of `srv-fill100-f1-r01`). Recorded as latent in the solid-phase batch too (`SOLID_PHASE_FLUID_SYSTEM_IMPLEMENTATION_REVIEW.md` item 2).

### 2.4 Mechanism C (state): retries warm-started from a cancelled or failed attempt

`RetainedSolver` keeps the step solver's last flows, heads and modes and its factorizations. After a hold the retry restarts the slice from the committed state but warm-starts from wherever the held attempt stopped. Measured on the vented three-tank chain: after two wall cuts the rate solve of the retry starts from 0.452 and -0.222 kg/s on tanks still at rest, converges linearly through the friction kink at zero flow and stops at the 20-iteration limit (`1.2e-6`) at every step size; a failed attempt never updates the carried flows, so every later retry fails the same way, at no cost (0.00 s of work each off-line; in game `1.954...E-6` repeated). It is also Problem 3's nondeterminism (`FLUID_STARTUP_HOLD_REVIEW.md`).

### 2.5 Mechanism D (policy): identical retries at the full budget, and a halving ladder from 100 ticks

A numerical hold kept its slice and was retried every cadence on the same state: with Mechanism C, the same failure forever; with a fresh solver, the same failure provably (the computation is identical). A wall hold halved the slice, but the fill's cost is front-loaded - the first 50 ms of the three-tank fill cost 858 ms on one warm thread (62 attempts, 197 Newton solves, 1,385 iterations, 15,159 property states) - so halving barely reduces it until the slice is a few ticks: in game every attempt of `fill100` was cut for the first 100 s (60 budget holds per 10 s with 12 workers busy), six levels of 2 s of discarded work per line.

### 2.6 Mechanism E (the model's limit): gas transfers cool the suction tank below the property domain

A pump moving 0.01 m3/s of nitrogen out of a closed, adiabatic 1 m3 tank expands its gas isentropically: 298.15 K at 101,325 Pa, 275.2 K at 76,561 Pa after 20 s, 273.1600 K at 74,594 Pa after 21.858 s, where the nitrogen property data and `FluidThermodynamics.state` end (273.16 K). Every trial beyond is refused as outside the domain, the step collapses (`advanced=1.858 of 5.0 s, accepted=492, rejected=532`), and the interval can never commit. This is **physics the model cannot reach**, not numerics: the pump keeps pulling (its head, 52 kPa, is far from 500 kPa and cannot reach it, since the discharge tank tops out near 2 atm), the tank has no heat exchange with its surroundings, and there is no state below 273.16 K. All three transfer probes stop at the same suction temperature.

### 2.7 What the pump model contributes

In every fill failure the pump is at `PUMP_TARGET` throughout (mode lists in the traces); the head-limit and shutoff switching is reached only at the end of the fill, where the solid-phase batch's rules R1 to R4 close it cleanly (every closed chain ends at 601,325 Pa with the pump `CLOSED` and certifies STEADY). Pump work and heating heat the discharge (321 K in the transfers), which is not where anything fails. So the pump model is not the cause of the held fills; it is the cause of the transfers' end (section 5).

## 3. The fix (T3, T4)

1. **Trace projection** (`ee050d4`, `PassiveStepSolver`, `SparseNewton`). A fluid amount at or below `1e-6` of its difference floor (1e-10 of a gas node's amount; a millionth of a resolved trace in a liquid-full node) no longer bounds `maximumStep`; the new `SparseNewton.Equations.project` hook keeps one percent of it in the trial instead - the share the limiter would have left it - and the rest of the step is taken as the direction says. The same rule already existed for solid-moment dust (`SOLID_CLAMP_FRACTION`). Conservation is untouched: inventories are rebuilt from the converged flows by `ConservativeTransport.reconstruct`. The default hook is a no-op, so nothing else changes.
2. **Velocity cap by driving pressure** (`55ba5a0`, `PassiveStepSolver.edgeRows`). A plain passive connection (no device, no filter) takes the cap of the end its driving pressure pushes from; at every root the flow has that sign, so no root moves and the row is continuous at zero flow. Devices and filters keep the flow's donor: applying the rule to a pump sent the dead-headed pump of `IslandCertificateTest` down a head-limit path it does not converge on (measured, `probes/DeadHeadProbeTest.java`), and a filter's cake-dependent coefficient was left as it was.
3. **Hold policy** (`5b3d903`, `IslandCoordinator.hold`, `IslandClock.deferRetry`, `ProcessSolveServices`). Every held attempt (no committed candidate, no refused transaction) installs a fresh `RetainedSolver`, so a retry is the same computation as the first attempt on the same committed state (Mechanism C). Because a numerical failure is then exactly reproducible, a retry of the same slice is pointless: every hold halves the slice to at least one tick (wall holds did before), and a hold at one tick defers the retry to `cadence * 2^k` ticks, k the holds in a row, at most `2^6` = 64 cadences (320 s at the default cadence). Budget holds - the round deadline, the wall budget, and a soft budget whose approximate fallback refused (now reported as `HELD: soft budget; approximate fallback refused: ...`) - follow the same ladder, since a warmer or less loaded worker can pass them later; a refused fallback's retry runs the full solve alone on the whole budget until an interval is accepted. A fence, a released fence, a resolved delivery or a property resume clears the wait and the count; a topology change makes a new island. Online ticks only, no wall clock in simulated time. New counters `budgetHolds`, `numericalHolds`, `retriesDeferred`.
4. **Cold-start slice** (`7f933ff`, `IslandCoordinator.topology`). An island a topology change created starts on a one-tick slice, doubled by each accepted full interval back to the cadence (1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, then the cadence). Whatever the player just built starts its transient in that island's first slice; a quiet island pays six short solves. Islands registered from a save or a checkpoint (P12, P31, the paced benchmarks) start at their cadence as before.

**No retry that would succeed is lost:** a numerical retry on the same state, slice and a fresh solver is bit for bit the failed computation; the halving changes the problem; the deferred retries still happen (at most one per 320 s) and any input change retries at once.

## 5. Pump model: proposal (owner's addition)

The analysis in section 2.7 is the basis: the pump's equations are not what held the fills, so nothing in the pump was changed for them. For gas transfers they are the cause, together with adiabatic vessels and the 273.16 K property floor: a prescribed suction volume flow limited only by a pressure rise keeps evacuating a closed gas tank, and with 1 m3 tanks the rise needed never reaches the limit. What a player sets on a pump today: **Suction flow (m3/s)** and **Max pressure rise (Pa)** (`FluidDeviceScreen`); efficiency is fixed at 1. There is no head curve: the model is a flow target clamped by a pressure-rise limit, with a closed state at shutoff and a velocity clamp (`PUMP_TARGET`, `PUMP_HEAD_LIMIT`, `CLOSED`, `PUMP_VELOCITY_LIMIT`).

| option | what it removes or changes in the equations | what a player sets | against a closed or full line | effect on tests and the regression reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1. Pressure rise scaled by suction density** (a head: `rise_max = setting * rho_suction / rho_ref`, `rho_ref` water at 298.15 K and 101,325 Pa) | nothing removed; the head-limit row's constant becomes a function of the suction density (one more Jacobian dependency) | the same two fields; "Max pressure rise" means "for water" - a meaning change | liquid: the same shutoff (601,325 Pa within water's compressibility, about 0.02 %); gas: a pump on nitrogen at 1 atm reaches its limit at about 580 Pa of rise, so a gas transfer stops within a second with the suction tank a fraction of a kelvin cooler | fills' shutoff moves by the density ratio (the exact 601,325 Pa assertions in `FilterBlockLineIslandTest`, `PUMP_SHUTOFF` fixtures and `FluidPumpedFillLineTest` move by up to ~100 Pa); regression chain-100 has no pump: unchanged; P12/P31 would move only if they pump gas |
| **P2. Suction floor** (the pump throttles so its suction stays above a fixed pressure, e.g. 50 kPa) | adds a mode (`PUMP_SUCTION_LIMIT`) to the active set: the opposite of a simplification | a new field, or a fixed floor | liquid fills unchanged; gas transfers stop at the floor - but an adiabatic tank starting at 298 K reaches 273.16 K at 74.6 kPa, so any floor below that still ends in the model's limit | new mode, new tests; no regression effect |
| **P3. A pump moves liquid only** (on a suction with no liquid phase above a threshold it is closed; it "loses prime") | adds a switch on the suction's phase; the flow target applies only to liquid suctions | the same fields; the device no longer moves gas (a compressor would be a separate device) | liquid fills unchanged; gas transfers never start, so nothing cools; a transfer of liquid out of a tank stops when the tank's bulk (homogeneous) withdrawal no longer carries liquid above the threshold | a threshold switch is exactly the kind of active-set boundary the solver finds hard; `IslandCertificateTest`'s pumped nitrogen loop and dead-headed pump, `VelocityClampTest`'s pumped cases and the crude pump GameTest pump gas or mixtures and would change |

The owner's own examples, checked against this model: "a prescribed volumetric or mass flow clamped by a maximum head, with no curve and no active set" is today's pump with a smoothed active set; the target-to-limit-to-closed switching did not fail anywhere (it closes every fill cleanly), and smoothing it would not stop a gas transfer from evacuating a closed tank. "A pump as a fixed pressure rise" removes the flow target (a control change) and would evacuate a gas tank faster. "Keeping the curve but removing the switching": there is no curve.

**Recommendation: P1**, a head in the physical sense, is the only option that removes the gas-transfer failure without adding a mode, and it matches what a real pump does. It changes the meaning of the "Max pressure rise" field (a rise for water, proportionally less for gas) and moves the fills' shutoff pressure slightly, so by the owner's rule it is **not implemented**; it is the owner's decision. Until then a gas transfer runs until its suction tank reaches 273.16 K (about 22 s for 1 m3 tanks at the default flow) and stays held there, retried at most once per 320 s. A heat exchange between vessels and their surroundings would also remove it but changes every tank's physics and is outside the pump.

## 7. Deviations and open items

1. **Worktree switch.** The session started in the coordinator's worktree; the agent switched into `agent-ae139e4fc1b184b36` with `EnterWorktree` as the brief asked, after a write hook refused cross-worktree edits. Nothing outside that worktree was written.

3. **Two changes beyond the brief's candidates**, both measured before being kept: the passive-connection velocity cap (a documented latent defect that turned out live) and the cold-start slice for topology-created islands (the answer to "whether the first dispatch should be spread", see `FLUID_STARTUP_HOLD_REVIEW.md`).

Cheaper integration of that event (error control at phase appearance, the modified-Newton refresh rule in stiff transients) would change solver numerics everywhere and is left as a follow-up.
5. **Not measured:** the client row, and a six-tank `fill100`; the probes cover the six-tank chains at 8 per run.
