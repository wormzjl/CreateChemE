## 0. Recommendations in brief

1. **Package strategy.** Formulation changes (direct liquid path, temperature-dependent interactions in the kernel, stability-driven phase count, PH/UV) are code and apply to every network package, including the bundled `createcheme:tjl20_methane_nitrogen`, whose fluid thermodynamic revision moves once (fresh world, chain-100 re-captured after validation). Pilot data (spines, Tr = 0.8 anchors, E-PPR78 kij, CO2, cryogenic, 10 MPa and 1200 K ranges) live in a separate bundled package `createcheme:pilot_cryogenic` with its own property records, selected in P3 only by a test (and dev datapack) override of the network record. The bundled network package is extended in P3 only through fields no pin hashes: nitrogen and methane to 10 MPa. Shared column records do not move; the neural binding, the column pins and the fluid presets hold.
2. **Direct liquid path.** `HydrocarbonModel.phase` evaluates the liquid at the state pressure; `REFERENCE_PRESSURE`, `GlobalLiquidResponse`, the `liquidCompressibility` config entry and the checkpoint's compressibility field are deleted; liquid water moves to IF97 Region 1 at the state pressure; the acoustic bound uses the phases' own compressibility; the legacy translation anchors are evaluated at their own pressure. The network Jacobian is a finite-difference block sweep, so nothing has to be chained by hand; values change in the volume, energy, equilibrium and edge rows, no block changes sign or sparsity.
3. **Anchors.** A new per-property-record field `volume_translation` (Tr = 0.8 saturated liquid from the Java oracle, with the oracle revision) in the pilot records; a gate test recomputes it from the oracle. The shared `liquid_calibration.json` points and the standard-density anchors stay for everything else, as declared estimates.
4. **E-PPR78.** kij(T) enters as a new kernel mixing plan with per-temperature pair values and their first and second temperature derivatives; the existing plans stay bitwise. The cuts keep their constant kij; no P3 fixture needs the Xu et al. 2015 rule. Validation is functional (GERG-2008 bubble points against the D7 K-value targets) because the open papers do not print the matrix; Table S4 remains the provenance to fetch.
5. **Spine wiring.** `TranslatedPengRobinson` gains an ideal-gas-function variant; the network stays on its sensible energy datum (formation enthalpy subtracted per component, carried by the `EnergyReference`); `ThermoIdentity` becomes the network's thermodynamic revision. Methane's conflict is three-way (JANAF, GERG-2008 ideal part, CEA); an ab initio line-list heat capacity (ExoMol) settles it.
6. **Phases.** The network's outer active-set check and the inventory refresh use the engine's TP service (stability test first) instead of `flashTP`'s Wilson substitution and the `vaporBranch` root heuristic; residuals keep direct phase evaluations. A second-order finish removes the near-critical NOT_CONVERGED outside the declared band; inside it a remaining failure is typed and the island holds.
7. **PH/UV.** The network needs UV (vessels) and PH (zero-holdup junctions) but solves both as coupled residuals; the engine implements PH and UV as bracketed outer loops over TP for initialization, the inventory refresh and the fixtures, and promises no equilibrium derivatives at critical singularities.
8. **G3.** Ten fixture families (F1 to F10, section 8) indexed by process, with D7 targets and named holdouts.
9. **Work.** Twelve work packages, about 150 hours, four stages; six can start in parallel in one worktree.

### 2.3 Node Jacobian

The network builds its Jacobian by finite differences: `PassiveStepSolver.Equations.differentiateEntries` (lines 1871-1960) perturbs each local column and re-assembles only the rows that see it through the same `nodeAccumulate`, `edgeRows` and `nodeRows` the residual uses. `hydrocarbonDerivatives` has no caller in the network (only `CubicPhaseEvaluator` line 128 and tests use `differentiate`). So there is no hand chain to rewrite. What changes is the function being differenced:

| Rows (`PhaseLayout.balanceRows` line 230, `equilibriumResidual` line 339; edge rows) | Columns | Old | New | Sign / shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Volume closure `(V - V_target)/V_target` | ln P | `-k v_L` per liquid mole | `dv/dP` of the EOS (`-kappa_EOS v`, 0.3 to 10 times the old value; P1: the global 1e-9 is 2 to 9 times smaller than cryogenic liquids' own) and Region 1's `dv/dP` | same sign, same sparsity |
| Volume closure | ln T | `dv_ref/dT exp(-k dP)` | `dv/dT` at the state | same |
| Energy `(U - U_target)/scale` | ln P, ln T | `(v - T v_T)` integral form | `dh/dP - v - P dv/dP` and `cp - P dv/dT` at the state | same |
| Equilibrium `ln(y/x) = ln phi_L - ln phi_V + ln(P/Pc)` | ln P | `-1/P + vbar_i,ref exp(-k dP)/RT` | `vbar_i/RT - 1/P` at the state | same |
| Water saturation `ln(p_w/psat)` | unchanged | | | |
| Edge rows through `Transport.velocityLimit` (lines 1699, 1917) | node T, P, amounts | constant `k` | `kappa` of each phase, a function of the node's T, P and x | already coupled to those columns; no sparsity change |

Determinism: fixed arithmetic order everywhere; the per-temperature prepared terms (`Prepared`, one solving thread) stay keyed by the exact temperature; the Region 1 workspace is thread-confined like the PR workspace; tests compare a reused workspace with fresh ones bitwise (as P2 section 5.1 does). Allocation: the liquid path allocates less (above); water adds compute (a 34-term Region 1 sum per trial instead of per temperature, about 0.3 us), measured through the regression harness's allocated-MB and wall columns on chain-100 and one fluid benchmark pair (60 s warm-up + 60 s, benchmark rule).

## 3. Volume-translation anchors

- **Field.** New optional object in a property record: `volume_translation` {`type`: `saturated_liquid_reduced_temperature`, `reduced_temperature`: 0.8, `temperature_kelvin`, `pressure_pascal` (the reference saturation pressure at that temperature), `molar_volume_m3_per_mol` (the reference saturated liquid volume), `reference` (fluid file and revision, `CoolProp dev/fluids @ ae81610`, the EOS citation), `oracle` (`science.thermo.reference.HelmholtzFluid`, commit of the oracle), `source`}. `MaterialCatalog.Property` gains it (null when absent, so no existing serialized form or fingerprint moves); the loader checks `temperature_kelvin = reduced_temperature x Tc` of the record's own PR78 constants within 1e-9 relative, positivity, and refuses a record with both `volume_translation` and a `liquid_calibration.json` point for a package that uses it (one anchor per species per package). - **Everything else.** Light ends C3 to C5 keep their NIST 2 MPa points (`liquid_calibration.json`), the cuts their standard-density anchors; both are declared estimates (P1 section 2.8: no anchor changes the picture that liquids are within about 2 % mean below Tr 0.9; the single rule is chosen for the pilot). ## 4. E-PPR78 kij(T)

### 4.1 Formula

With `alpha_ik` the fraction of the groups of molecule i that are of type k (every pilot species is one group: N2, CH4, C2H6, CO2), `A_kl`, `B_kl` in Pa, `a_i(T)` the Soave PR78 attraction and `b_i` the co-volume of the kernel:

    E_ij(T) = -1/2 sum_k sum_l (alpha_ik - alpha_jk)(alpha_il - alpha_jl) A_kl (298.15/T)^(B_kl/A_kl - 1)
    k_ij(T) = [E_ij(T) - (sqrt(a_i)/b_i - sqrt(a_j)/b_j)^2] / [2 sqrt(a_i a_j)/(b_i b_j)]

For two single-group molecules `E_ij = A_12 f(T)` with `f = (298.15/T)^(B/A - 1)`. The equivalent cross term is the cleaner form to implement and differentiate:

    a_ij(T) = sqrt(a_i a_j)(1 - k_ij) = (a_i b_j/b_i + a_j b_i/b_j)/2 - (b_i b_j/2) E_ij(T)
    da_ij/dT = (a_i' b_j/b_i + a_j' b_i/b_j)/2 - (b_i b_j/2) E_ij'   with  f' = -(B/A - 1) f / T
    d2a_ij/dT2 = (a_i'' b_j/b_i + a_j'' b_i/b_j)/2 - (b_i b_j/2) E_ij''  with  f'' = (B/A - 1)(B/A) f / T^2

and `k_ij = 1 - a_ij/sqrt(a_i a_j)` with its derivatives by the quotient rule. The kernel's constants (Omega_a 0.45724, Omega_b 0.07780, `PengRobinsonKernel` lines 119-123) differ from E-PPR78's 0.457235529 and 0.0777960739 by 1e-5 and 5e-5 relative; the implementation uses the kernel's own `a_i`, `b_i` (self-consistent) and records the difference. The Soave `a_i` reaches zero at Tr = (1 + 1/m)^2 (N2 about 1390 K, CO2 1776 K), above the 1200 K ceiling; `TranslatedPengRobinson.prepare` already refuses a zero root (line 263).

### 6.2 P3 design

- **Regime oracle.** `flashTP` becomes an adapter over `FluidTpEquilibrium` on the package's contract: stability test first (P1: below 15 us per node at 21 components), then the split. A converged single-phase node's check becomes one stability test instead of a flash, which is cheaper than today's.
- **Free water in the engine** (P2 open item). The engine reproduces the network's rule exactly, or the flash and the residual rows disagree: hydrocarbon liquid at P, hydrocarbon vapour at its partial pressure `pc = P - p_w`, ideal steam sharing the gas volume, liquid water from Region 1 at P, `p_w = psat` when both water phases exist (`FluidThermodynamics.state` lines 213-241, `flashTP` lines 262-283, `PhaseLayout` rows lines 340-353). The outer loop over `pc` stays as in `flashTP`; `TangentPlaneStability` gains a per-branch pressure (vapour-like trials at `pc`, liquid-like at P), which reduces bitwise to P1's test without water. Refusals stay D8's (hydrates, water-chemistry species, water below 273.16 K).
- **Second-order finish** (P2 section 9). When accelerated substitution has run 10 steps with the dominant eigenvalue estimate above 0.9, or after 50 steps, switch to Newton on the vapour mole numbers minimizing the Gibbs energy: gradient `ln f_i^V - ln f_i^L`, Hessian from the kernel's analytic `d ln phi/dn` of both phases (`d ln f_i/dn_j = delta_ij/n_i - 1/N + (1/N) dLogPhiDn[i][j]` per phase, the kernel's row being per mole), step bounded to keep amounts positive, line search on the Gibbs energy; converged at `max |ln f^V - ln f^L| < 1e-10`. The same machinery as the stability test's Newton. Target: zero NOT_CONVERGED outside the declared band on P2's 130,600 binary scan states and the new pilot scans.
- **Declared critical band.** Pure fluids: plan section 3 widened band (Tr 0.95-1.1 x Pr 0.8-1.5, liquid Tr 0.90-0.95 x Pr 0.58-0.74, dense supercritical Tr 1.05-1.2 x Pr 2-3), applied when one component holds at least 0.95 of the amount. Mixtures, from the engine's own results rather than weighted critical constants: a two-phase result with `sum_i z_i (ln K_i)^2 < 0.01` (short tie line), or a single-phase result whose stability test ended on a trial within 100 times the trivial distance of the feed (incipient phase next to the feed). The thresholds are calibrated on the P2 near-critical scan so that its 122 failures and their neighbours fall inside, and the band's coverage of the scan is reported. Inside the band: grade research-only; a result that still does not converge is NOT_CONVERGED with a `CRITICAL_BAND` diagnostic; the network turns it into the existing held-island path (typed reason, inventory kept, published on the engine schedule). No split is fabricated and no single phase is reported as stable when the test said unstable.
- **Merging.** A converged two-phase node whose split falls below `sum z_i (ln K_i)^2 < 1e-8` (the P2 collapse criterion) is merged into one phase by the outer check and re-tested; the network's equilibrium rows are then removed with the phase.
- **Appearance and disappearance.** Appearance: the outer check's stability test on single-phase nodes (as today's flash, now with a proof). Disappearance: unchanged, through the step limiter and the outer check when a phase amount reaches zero.

## 7. TP, PH and UV

- **What the network needs.** Vessels close internal energy and volume: `PhaseLayout.balanceRows` rows `(U - U_target)` and `(V - V_target)` (lines 230-236): UV. - **What P3 implements** in `FluidTpEquilibrium` (the `ph` and `uv` stubs, lines 217-236):

  - Use: `InventoryEquilibrium` may call `uv` for its regime decision; the closed-vessel fixtures (F8), presentation and later consumers call them. They never run inside a network residual.
- **Derivatives at critical singularities** (plan section 4: no promise of finite smooth derivatives there). ## 10. Risks

- **Fluid gameplay pins.** chain-100 moves (section 2.5); `FluidThermoDomainHoldTest` depends on 0.24 K of EOS suction cooling; the pump rise limit moves by about 0.1 % with the reference density; the domain messages at 2 MPa for N2 and CH4 change. - **Liquid-full stiffness.** Real liquid compressibility ranges from about 5e-10 /Pa (heavy cuts, stiffer than the old 1e-9) to far above it near a critical point, so liquid-full nodes' pressure response and the acoustic velocity clamp change; line-search backtracks and `jacobianBlockFallbacks` may rise, especially with the new "liquid root absent" refusal. Watched through the regression counters and one benchmark pair.
- **Metastable water.** If IF97's release does not support Region 1 below saturation, the admission of section 2.2 needs another bound (the envelope's minimum or a declared margin); decided in WP4 with the citation.
- **Determinism.** New per-trial work (Region 1 at P, stability in the outer check, second-order finish) must stay order-fixed and allocation-free in iterations; every new path gets a reused-versus-fresh-workspace bitwise test.
- **Cost.** Budgets (D7: the owner's benchmark practice): per-node stability <= 15 us at 21 components (P1); TP flash p95 <= 50 us at 21 components two-phase (P2: 37-43 us); chain-100 wall and allocated MB within +10 % of the pre-P3 run on the same machine (the counters of `SolverRegressionHarness`, not wall time under load); one fluid benchmark pair (60 s + 60 s) before WP11. No work enters the tick loop: every change sits in residual evaluation or the outer active-set pass, which run on the island workers.
- **E-PPR78 provenance.** Without Table S4 the pilot kij are a third-party transcription validated only functionally; if S4 differs later, the interaction record's revision and the pilot fingerprint change (no gameplay pin depends on it in P3). Above E-PPR78's fitted range kij(T) grows (CH4/CO2 0.30 at 900 K, C2H6/N2 -0.15, appendix A); the residual is small at low pressure but unreferenced at high T and P: declared.
- **Methane source.** An incomplete line list at 1200 K would leave methane at its declared 3.5 %.
- **Dense-fluid transport.** The network's viscosity is a reference-pressure approximation ("Reference-pressure transport approximation", `MixtureViscosity` line 9; `ViscosityCorrelation` lines 68-72 evaluate at the table's pressure). At 10 MPa dense or supercritical states (N2, CH4 in `net`; all pilot species) the viscosity is a dilute or saturated-liquid value, so pipe flow rates there are estimates, and the PIP slot flip switches mixing rules. - **Stability trial set.** Above 8 components only the two Wilson trials run (P1 limit); a liquid-liquid split reachable from neither would be missed; unchanged from P1, noted.
- **CO2 in the 10 MPa window.** CO2's band (288.9-334.5 K x 5.90-11.07 MPa) covers most of its supercritical states below 10 MPa near ambient: much of the pilot's supercritical CO2 is research-only by construction.
