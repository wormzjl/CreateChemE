## 3. Decisions taken by the maintainer (do not reopen without asking)

- Side strippers will **not** be modelled. The literature preset keeps its dew-point warning as a documented
  property of the reconstruction.
- A tray below the water dew point is a warning, never a rejection.
- Draw specifications above ~0.4× the literature molar rates without pumparounds are invalid inputs, not solver bugs.
- VDU: literature before experiments. **Case A = Ji & Bagajewicz, IECR 2002 41:6094/6100 adopted** (2026-09-08).

## 4. Next work: VDU case A

Plan: `V3_VDU_CASE_A_PLAN.md` (worktree documentation, copied to the main checkout). Evidence:
`V3_VDU_LITERATURE_AND_THERMO_DATA.md`. Key facts for whoever picks it up:

- No new topology is needed: zero reflux, zero-duty sump with steam, duty pumparounds and rate draws are already
  legal; node 0 becomes the ejector precondenser at the published 127 °C.
- WP0 contract (assumptions A1 top 10.6 kPa / 0.5 kPa per tray, A2 feed tray 6 of 7, A3 steam 150 °C, A4 sharp
  residue cut at 73.6 vol %) → WP1 package `createcheme:vdu_lc36_dwsim` via the DWSIM pipeline (floor 500 Pa,
  Maxwell-Bonnell bias advisory) → WP2 geometric pressure schedule, cold-start race, overflash and precondenser
  audits → WP3 qualification with wide bands → WP4 preset/GUI → WP5 optional MB-anchored refit.
- Thermo caveat: PR78 as characterized sits 4–13 K below Maxwell-Bonnell at 1–13 kPa (K-values 14–25 % high at
  13 kPa); validated implementations in `build/pkgcmp/vdu-lit/psat-check.mjs` and `psat-validate.mjs`.
- Environment: the system `python` is the Microsoft Store stub; the DWSIM verification scripts need a real
  CPython 3 with numpy. `csc.exe` (.NET 4) compiles the DWSIM helpers. Node 22 is available (used for the PDF
  extraction and the vapour-pressure check).
