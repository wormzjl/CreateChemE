Plan: `PHASE_PORTS_PLAN.md` in this folder. Entries are dated; a later entry supersedes an earlier one only by saying so.

## Owner decisions (2026-09-26)

- **D1 Liquid-only pump.** The centrifugal pump is a placeholder that handles liquid only, possibly with some solids. A pump whose inlet is gas plus liquid (two-phase) or gas gives an error.
  - Behaviour proposed to the owner, pending O1: the pump goes to a CLOSED mode with a typed reason shown in the GUI, and the island keeps running. It is not an island hold, because a hold freezes every other line on the island.
- **D2 Gas compressor.** Add a compressor for gas: gas-only inlet, with the same error rule for a liquid or two-phase inlet.
- **D3 Tank outlets with a specified phase.** Top output = gas, middle output = mixed (today's bulk withdrawal), bottom output = liquid. Inputs are not distinguished.
- **D4 Suction density only.** Pump calculations and their verification use the input (suction) density only; the in/out density difference is small and immaterial.
  - The rise limit keeps its suction-density scaling, `riseLimit = maximumAddedPressure * rho_suction / pumpReferenceDensity` (`PassiveStepSolver.java:1051` at 9674bf1).
- **D5 One-slice delay accepted.** Events may be seen one 5 s slice late. The fluid engine is moving to a backward-Euler basis with 5 s slices (a separate plan covers that). Nothing may be designed that needs sub-slice event timing.

## Defaults taken by the plan unless the owner objects (reversible)

- **A1 Faces.** On the single-block tank: UP face = top (gas), DOWN face = bottom (liquid), horizontal faces = middle (mixed). No per-face configuration in v1.
- **A2 Bottom outlet contents.** The bottom outlet draws hydrocarbon liquid, free water and mobile solids together. No water/oil decant.
- **A3 What the inlet check reads.** The source stream the suction line delivers, not the pump's own inlet junction. There is no cavitation error; flashing at the inlet only lowers the suction density and so the rise limit.
- **A4 Thresholds** (plan Appendix B):
  - ports open at 1 % of the vessel volume, and the throttle lands them at 0.5 %;
  - the pump refuses above 2 % vapour by volume and resumes below 0.5 %;
  - the compressor refuses above 1 % condensed by mass and resumes below 0.2 %.
- **A5 When the inlet decision is taken.** Wrong-phase at each slice start on the committed state, with hysteresis carried in the committed endpoint mode. Empty suction at each step start, following the port.
- **A6 What stays bulk.** Module withdrawals and generator supplies stay bulk.
- **A7 Compressor and solids.** The compressor refuses any solid population above the trace volume fraction.

## WP1 defaults (2026-09-26, implementation; reversible, recorded in `PHASE_PORTS_REVIEW.md` WP1)

- **A9 What an absent phase stream is, for every reader.** A phase port whose phase the vessel does not hold (VAPOR: no gas volume; LIQUID: no hydrocarbon liquid and no free water) reads as the node's bulk stream with a **zero velocity limit**: its density (A8), viscosity, specific enthalpy, composition and solid share are the bulk's, and its velocity cap is a zero mass flow. So every column and sign test is decided as for a bulk end, a capped connection carries nothing out through it, and an iterate that still moves mass through it moves the bulk's composition and enthalpy, which keeps every Newton target conservative. The reconstruction books such an outflow as a bulk one, `PipeTransfer` samples it as bulk, and the solid mobility check treats it as a bulk end. WP2's availability closure then keeps these ports shut for outflow; A9 only makes the absent stream well defined in the meantime.
- **A10 A closed connection draws nothing.** A passive connection the boundary rule has closed (`boundaryClosed`, row `f = q`) carries exactly zero at every iterate, so it neither builds its node's phase stream nor lifts that node's structural-zero drop. Exact (every term it would add is multiplied by a zero flow); it makes a closed phase port bitwise inert (`PhasePortTest.closedPhasePortsLeaveTheBulkLineBitwise`).
- **A11 Sites beyond the plan's lists routed through the end stream.** The balanced junction seed (its neighbours' pressures and the mixture they deliver) and the vessel trial seeds of `initialPhaseSeeds` read the donor end's stream and end pressure, like the Appendix A readers; the boundary-reopen band is taken on the two end pressures. All are bitwise for BULK ends.
- **A12 Certificate digest version.** The graph-identity digest gains one integer per pipe end (the port ordinal) and its header becomes `createcheme-certificate-graph-identity-2`; every saved certificate signature changes (no compatibility work: a stale certificate is discarded on load and the island keeps its inventory).

## WP2 defaults (2026-09-26, implementation; reversible, recorded in `PHASE_PORTS_REVIEW.md` WP2)

- **A13 The availability mask.** A phase port may carry outflow in a step iff its phase fills at least `phi_open` = 0.01 of its vessel on the accepted state the step starts from (VAPOR: `vaporVolume/V`; LIQUID: `(liquidVolume + waterVolume)/V`; `V` the state's volume). One mask per step (`PassiveStepSolver.closedPorts`, per node, one bit per port kind some connection has there), read by every `boundaryAllowed` caller - the start closures, the pass loop, the reachable components, the start point, both seeds, the approximation probe and the boundary-reopen test - and part of the workspace and cycle key. Only ports some connection has are recorded, so an all-BULK graph's mask is all zero (bitwise). Inflow through a port is never refused.
- **A14 The throttle.** `(phi_start - phi_reserve) * V_start * rho_stream,start / (n * dt)` for the direction leaving an open port, `min`-ed with the velocity cap in `edgeRows`' saturated law. `dt` is the solve's step (the backward-Euler step the interval solver attempts, truncated to the interval's remainder, halved on retry), never the slice; `rho` the drawn stream's start density; `n` the vessel's ends of that phase port whose connection may carry outflow by the static rule (not by the start closures, so `n` is a constant of the solve). Off in rate solves and, like the filter's room limit, while any device of the island holds `PUMP_TARGET` (WP3 revisits it). A throttled connection's accepted mode stays PASSIVE.
- **A15 Start point inside the throttle.** A pass's start flows are clamped to the throttle, as `initialMassFlow` starts inside the velocity cap (measured need: a 5 s step started at the cap, 67 times the throttle, failed its first Newton).
- **A16 Landing under the stateless rule.** A draining port closes at the first step start below `phi_open`; the throttle is a per-step floor at `phi_reserve`, landing a phase on the reserve only where one step spans the band. A port fed while it drains duty-cycles about `phi_open` (measured, benign). Hysteresis carried in the committed endpoint mode is offered to the owner (review WP2 section 10, option 1b), not implemented.

## D9 defaults (2026-09-26, implementation; reversible, recorded in `PHASE_PORTS_REVIEW.md` D9)

- **A17 What the head reads.** `h = g * m_c * H / V` at a LIQUID port of a RESERVOIR or PORT node, zero at every other end. `m_c` is `FluidThermodynamics.liquidMass` of the state the caller states a pressure for: hydrocarbon liquid, free water **and every solid**, as the review's formula writes it, so a vessel holding solids without a liquid also has a head (none of the existing fixtures has one). The alternative, a head only while a liquid is present (dry solids transmitting no hydrostatic pressure to the port), puts a jump of `g m_s H/V` into the row where the liquid appears or vanishes inside a Newton; not taken. `V` is the vessel's inventory volume (its identity), not the trial state's EOS volume; `H` = 1 m (`PassiveStepSolver.LEVEL_HEAD_HEIGHT`).
- **A18 No certificate digest change.** H is a constant and V and the LIQUID port are already in `graphIdentity` (A12), so the digest stays `...-graph-identity-2`.

## Owner decisions after D9 and the extreme-topology tests (2026-09-26, later)

- **D11 Ports carry mixed phases by priority (supersedes the "outlet phase absent" closure of plan 3.4, WP2, and A2).** Every port can carry more than one phase; which phases and in what order is a priority: a **bottom (LIQUID) port draws the heaviest condensed phase first** (the denser of hydrocarbon liquid and free water, with the solids of the drawn liquid), then the lighter liquid, then gas; a **top (VAPOR) port draws gas first**, then the lighter liquid by density, then the heavier; a **side (BULK) port draws the bulk** as today. A phase is drawn only up to what the vessel holds at the step start (the WP2 throttle becomes the primary phase's per-step capacity, zero reserve), and flow beyond that capacity draws the next phase in the order, within the same step ("priority stream", the second of the two implementations put to the owner: a per-step switch alone would trade the open/closed chatter for a liquid/gas chatter). Consequences: no port ever closes for want of a phase, so the availability mask, the 1 %/0.5 % band, the port refusal in `boundaryAllowed` and its reopen allowance go; `INLET_EMPTY` (plan 3.6) goes, a pump fed from a dry bottom port sees gas and is refused by D1's rule; a liquid-full vessel overflows through its vent instead of pressurising (plan 3.4 "Liquid-full vessels" is withdrawn); the absent-stream default A9 is replaced by the fallback phase's stream. The bottom-port order is a decant (A2 is withdrawn).
- **D12 Generators are one-way from the first pass.** A generator only pushes when its pressure allows and never receives (the standing rule of `boundaryAllowed`, now required of the cold start too): the first-interval pass-0 defect of `EXTREME_TOPOLOGY_TESTS.md` (multi-generator islands with different pressures fail at the default velocity cap because pass 0 treats every generator as two-way) is fixed by giving the cold-start seed and the first pass one-way generators (options O2 then O1 of that document, in that order; O3-O5 only if those leave cases failing). The owner notes that a player can replace generators with tanks at a higher pressure; the generator case stays the test.
- **D13 Vent gate defect: option A, polish before refusing (owner, 2026-09-26).** The reconstruction's equation-gate failures of `PHASE_PORTS_REVIEW.md` "Vent gate defect" (a Newton on a reused chord Jacobian stopping inside its tolerance on one balance row, magnified by the vessel's total-to-gas mole ratio in the volume and saturation rows) are fixed by option A: when the gate refuses a step, one re-solve from the converged point on a fresh Jacobian at 1e-2 x the tolerance, then the gate again; this runs only after a refusal, so every step accepted today stays bitwise. Options B-E (no preconditioner reuse across a dt change, per-component row scaling, re-flash in the reconstruction, accept) rejected.

## D11 defaults (2026-09-26, implementation; reversible, recorded in `PHASE_PORTS_REVIEW.md` D11)

- **A20 Solids go with the liquids in proportion to their volumes.** A vessel's solids are a uniform suspension in its combined liquid (the carrier the solid mobility check already reads: both liquids' volume-averaged density and viscosity), so the hydrocarbon-liquid and free-water phase streams each carry their volume share of every population; the two partition WP1's condensed stream, and with one liquid held its stream is that stream to the bit. The alternative (all solids with the heavier liquid, settled at the bottom) makes a top port drawing the heavier liquid last carry them all; not taken.
- **A21 N and the order.** `N` in `c = m_start/(N dt)` counts the vessel's phase-port ends whose connection may carry outflow from it by the static rule (not blocked, not into a generator: WP2's `n`, now over both port kinds, since each phase is shared by every phase port): an end facing a generator takes no share. The priority order and the capacities are read on the step's start state (constants of the solve); the heavier liquid by the liquids' own densities (no solids), water when not both are held. A rate solve has infinite capacities (a port draws its first held phase).
- **A22 The last phase is uncapped.** The last phase of a port's order the vessel holds takes whatever the earlier ones do not: a port's total flow is limited by the hydraulics and its velocity cap, not by the capacities (no throttle survives D11). The alternative, capping the total at `sum c` (the WP2 throttle's form), keeps a saturated law in every phase-port row for a limit a real step rarely meets; not taken.
- **A23 Pinned phases at the start state.** A phase drawn at its capacity is drawn at the step's start-state composition, enthalpy and density (it takes exactly its share of what the vessel held); the marginal phase at the end state (WP1's implicit stream; with nothing pinned the stream is exactly the phase's). Measured need: drawn at the end state, a phase drawn to zero ends on its own boundary and the pass loop cycled at every step size.
- **A24 Mixture properties and the velocity cap.** Composition, solids and specific enthalpy mass-flow-weighted; density the no-slip mixture's (mass-weighted specific volume); viscosity the phases' volume-flow average (the solver's bulk rule; the mass-weighted form would give a mostly-gas stream a liquid's viscosity); the velocity cap a volume flow `A v_min`, `v_min` the smallest velocity limit of the segment's phases, `limit = C + rho_m (A v_min - sum c/rho)` (the pinned phases at capacity, the marginal filling the rest), or the pinned mixture scaled to it where the pinned phases alone exceed it. BULK and single-phase ends keep `rho A v` to the bit.
- **A25 The segment.** Which phases are pinned and which is marginal is frozen per active-set pass (decided from the pass's start flows and seeds) and re-decided by the pass loop from the converged flows (an active-set change). It is part of the cycle key (`PassKey`), not of the workspace key: it changes values, not structure, and a workspace kept across a re-decision keeps its preconditioner.
- **A26 A pass starts inside its mixture's cap.** A start flow beyond its segment's velocity cap is moved to it (the start point's existing rule, applied to the priority stream; measured need: 137.7 kg/s of start flow against an 8.4 kg/s cap stalled the Newton). Ends drawing the phase the start estimate read are unchanged (bitwise).
- **A27 Drained seeds after a failed Newton.** When a pass's Newton fails and the phase correction of its iterate changes nothing, a vessel whose pinned segments draw a phase's whole start content is reseeded with its seed amounts less that content, flashed at the seed's T and P, if that changes its phase regime (measured need: nitrogen vented to zero from a tank filled with water; refused at every step size without it). Passes that converge are untouched.
- **A28 Static readers read the leading phase.** Columns, sign tests, start flows, `closeDeadHeads`, `illegalWithEitherDensity`, `reopenable`, the junction seeds and the solid mobility check read the stream of the first phase of the port's order the state holds (the priority stream at a vanishing flow). The mobility check asks the `GAS`, `ORGANIC_LIQUID` or `WATER` outlet of that phase (identical to WP1's `MIXED` for a vessel holding one liquid).
- **A29 No port closes.** `boundaryAllowed` is the static rule only; the reopen test reads it (no port allowance); nothing refuses outflow through a phase port.
- **A30 Unbacked traces are not booked.** A frozen stream books nothing of a component its donor neither holds (inventory exactly zero) nor receives in the step (no inflow, no injection): the seed's entry trace would otherwise be booked out of an inventory of zero (a WP1 defect D11 exposed: a methane tank's VAPOR port into a junction a nitrogen tank also feeds refused every step as a negative reconstruction on the WP1-D9 tree too). Exact: donor and receiver book the same fractions.
- **A31 A vessel holding none of the phases draws the bulk** (replacing A9's bulk with a zero velocity limit); A9's absent stream is otherwise the next phase of the order.
- **A32 One `LevelHeadTest` fixture restated (for the owner to confirm).** `drainOnTheHead` (5 s, 0.1 s) asserted WP2's landing (port closed below 1 %, 0.99300 % / 0.99716 %, one transition, the vessel keeping its water); under D11 the head drains the water below 1e-6 of the vessel (85 s / 77.2 s) and the drained tank passes no more than its trace plus 1e-6 kg/s of condensate. The head assertions (drain on the head alone, no reopen, BULK control) are unchanged.

## WP3/WP4 defaults (2026-09-26, implementation; reversible, recorded in `PHASE_PORTS_REVIEW.md` WP3 + WP4)

- **A37 What a phase port supplies to the inlet check (answers D11 open item 6).** The D11 priority draw of the device's target volume over the slice, `Q T`, on the committed state: each phase of the port's order up to `V_phase / N` (the D11 capacity over the slice), the last held phase with the rest; the leading phase when there is no target volume (`Q = 0`, or a rate solve with no slice). A dry bottom port reads gas, a bottom port holding less liquid than one slice of the target reads the gas it would break through to, a condensate trace does not read as liquid. Alternative: the leading phase always (a trace reads liquid; the pump then sucks gas for a slice); not taken.
- **A38 Where the refusal is decided.** By the interval solver at the start of each slice (`solve`, `solveApproximate`, before the solid event integrator's dispatch), on the slice's input graph and the committed endpoint modes (restated by `replayStart`, advanced to each returned interval; the rule is idempotent on one state, so a job's repeated trials agree), with the slice duration in the draw. The step solver holds the refusals for every step, rate seed and segment of the slice. A step solver no interval drives decides at each solve on its input states and carried modes, the step as the slice.
- **A39 A refused mover is the existing closed device.** `boundaryClosed` with mode `INLET_WRONG_PHASE` from the start of every solve of the slice (after the both-way-blocked closure): flow row, no pass reopens it, no carry restates it, nothing is reachable through it; the approximation probe skips its pump feasibility test. Bitwise the island with the device closed both ways (measured).
- **A40 The walk.** The device's own junction is crossed if its one other connection is passive; later junctions while they have two connections and no actuator (a filter edge is passive); a branch, dead-end or actuated junction supplies its state mixture; a generator or void supplies its state as BULK; a device on an evacuated first node is not decided. The reason names the node id where the walk ended.
- **A41 The compressor's suction pressure** is the first node's pressure (not its port's driving pressure with the level head), in the rise limit, the work, the carry, the start point, the pass loop and the reconstruction (the head is nonzero only where the inlet check refuses a compressor).
- **A42 Target above supply.** The initial mode compares `Q rho_s` with each suction-walk link's velocity cap on its donor end's stream and refuses a link that may not carry flow towards the device; the D11 capacities never bound a port's total (A22) and do not enter.
- **A43 Codec before WP5.** A `Compressor` control throws on the two checkpoint writers until format 6 gives it tag 3 (no runtime path builds one before WP5); `INLET_WRONG_PHASE` (ordinal 9) and `Kind.COMPRESSOR` (ordinal 7) are appended, so existing ordinals keep their meaning.
- **A44 Migration choices (C.1).** Each C test's compressor settings reproduce its old case where the physics allows (`NetworkRegimeTest` ratio 1.5 = the 500 kPa bracket, identical doubles; the sonic case ratio 6; dead-headed ratio 3 against 8 bar; RCPR ratio 1.01, the plan's lowest, near the pump's 575 Pa), keep test names, and keep the old tolerances where the old assertion's quantity survives (3 Pa landing, 0.05 Pa held head); `TransientQualificationTest` "pump against pressure" now transfers (ratio 3) where the 575 Pa pump was dead-headed; B re-baselines: `PumpJunctionStartupTest` crude generator and receiver at 150 kPa (bubble point 117.8 kPa by flash), the crude GameTest generator at 1 MPa (bubble point 226.0 kPa at 350 K), `FluidFallbackQualificationTest` "wet crude pump" drawing water at 2e-6 m3/s into the wet-crude tank.
- **A45 Fixture 4's "bit for bit".** Asserted against the same island with the refused line's device closed both ways (bitwise at 5 s and 0.1 s) and against the line alone (bitwise at 5 s; 1e-13 at 0.1 s, where a closed device's head unknown already moves the Newton's roundoff by up to 10 ulp).
