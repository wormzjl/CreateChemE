"Ledger against own inventory": the change of an island's net boundary ledger, per component, divided by that island's inventory of the component, which is the budget's reference (1e-6). The plain ledger ratio `wp2cmp.js` prints (relative to the island's largest net ledger component) reads 9.3e-6 for tol7-r01 because a through-flow island's net ledger is only its accumulation, about 1e-3 of its throughput; the absolute deviations there are 1e-8 to 6e-7 mol against about 100 mol that passed through the island.
