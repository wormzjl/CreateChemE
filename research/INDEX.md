# Research index

Local, git-ignored research material: datasets, literature sources, study harnesses and their journals. Write-ups of a batch of work live in `documentation/<batch>/` (see [../documentation/INDEX.md](../documentation/INDEX.md)); this folder holds what those documents were computed from.

Folder names are kept as they are: the tracked `MATERIALS.md` cites `research/viscosity-tools/`, `research/crude-assays/appearances.json` and `research/crude-regrouping/notes/CRUDE_ASSAY_CONVERSION.md`, and `research/crude-regrouping/relocation-manifest.json` hashes files by their current paths. Group new material by batch: one folder per batch (`YYYY-MM-DD-topic/` for new ones), then add a row here with its status.

Status words as in the documentation index: Implemented (date on `main`), In progress since, Planned, Concluded (research only), Abandoned / Superseded.
